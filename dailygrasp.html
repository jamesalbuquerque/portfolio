Case study 05 · Product thinking

DailyGrasp

A daily read designed to let you leave.

Role
Product, UX and AI-assisted build
Studio
Workchop, co-founder
Platforms
iOS and Android
Status
Launching soon

The idea

DailyGrasp sends one notification a day, at a time you choose, with one short read. You pick the topics and how deep to go in each: Beginner, Intermediate or Expert. Then the read ends. No feed, no infinite scroll, no algorithm chasing your attention.

The read

Every read has the same shape: a hook, five things to know, why it matters and key facts, readable in under two minutes. Articles are scored on popularity and depth to match your level. Built with Expo for iOS and Android.
